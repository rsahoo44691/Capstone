MSAI 699 Capstone Project

Predicting Heart Disease Risk

Building, testing, and correcting a clinical decision-support model
Rajesh Kumar Sahoo University of the Cumberlands Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence August 2026

What this talk shows

A model that predicts heart disease risk from 13 clinical measurements, and the testing that proved one of its earlier conclusions wrong. Repeated cross-validation and paired significance tests showed the accuracy ranking between models was noise. Error analysis showed every mistake sat in one narrow probability band, which turned two missed diagnoses into a threshold problem with a fix.
